  \textbf{Abstract.}
  We give exact all-dimension formulae or recurrences for five short and difficult
  entries of the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences: \seq{A000530},
  \seq{A003167}, \seq{A005787}, \seq{A006545}, and
  \seq{A007234}.  The methods combine run-composition counting, finite
  Egyptian-fraction recursion, affine-slice
  inclusion-exclusion, unlabelled cycle constructions, and weighted dynamic
  programming on permutation power-map functional graphs.  The last method
  extends from squaring to every fixed map \(\sigma\mapsto\sigma^d\).
  We also record exact terms and corrections for a further collection of OEIS
  entries.  Each computational statement is paired
  with an explicit witness, an exhaustive partition of the search, or an exact
  arithmetic checker.  In particular, the
  results include corrected values for \seq{A007234} and \seq{A002887}, the
  exact value \(\seq{A003167}(7)=155068098\), five exact values of
  \seq{A075099}, and \(\seq{A337433}(7)=87\).

  \vspace{0.55em}
  \textbf{Keywords:} integer sequences; exact enumeration; Burnside's lemma;
  functional graphs; unlabelled generating functions; Egyptian fractions;
  computational certificates.
